## What the owner must do (the taps the agent could not make)

With this build, on export-suite as an on-device board:

1. EXP-33: Export anything, close the share sheet with its × button: no error, nothing written.
2. EXP-26 / EXP-27: export-large PNG x2 Full Page, page 1, then all pages; close the sheet: one toast "Image was reduced to 7905 × 6324 to fit" (EXP-27: 6455 × 5164).
3. EXP-39: with a free account (or `subscription_expires_at` 0), export on-device 6 times, closing the sheet each time: exports 1-5 deliver, the 6th shows Export Limitations.
4. EXP-42: EXP-13 → Save to Files (one folder) and EXP-10 → Save to Files; open a PNG and the PDF in Files; select text in the PDF.
5. EXP-37 needs a second account.

## Observations

- **The free-cap count and the usage event come after the share sheet closes, not when it opens.** In `lekhboard-capacitor/lekh/src/board/export/deliver.ts`, `handOff` awaits `shareFiles` and only then increments `num_offline_export_done` and sends the usage event. The comment there says "Counted at presentation". On iOS, `shareFiles` resolves only when the sheet is dismissed (`PluginImpl.mm` `shareFiles` → `AppGlobal shareDoc:` `completionWithItemsHandler`). So if the app is killed while the sheet is up (by the user or by the system), that export is never counted and no event is sent. The agent saw this on 6 out of 6 exports. Expected (C++, `ExportInputBuilder.cpp:119-141`): the export counts once it is handed off. Severity is low (a user has to force-quit from the sheet).
- Raster exports are quick on this phone: a 7-page PNG export reached the sheet in about 4 s, and EXP-27's three 33 MP pages in about 9 s. The slow `toBlob` seen on Android does not show here.
- The PDF modal reads "Downloading fonts (0.0 MB)…" on iOS too while the native `getFontData` calls run (EXP-30, EXP-45).
- With the network cut, a doc-list navigation in the same session went to the sign-in screen. A relaunch online restored the session; the stored refresh token was kept.
- Delivered exports stay in the app's `tmp/export/<job>/` (and `tmp/fonts/` after PDF exports) until the system cleans them up. A cancelled job deletes its files.

## Differences from the Android (android-export-2026-10-23) and web (web-export-2026-10-23) results

- Every file case that ran passes on iOS, as on Android and on web Chromium. iOS raster sizes equal the web sizes (no devicePixelRatio factor), so EXP-16 is not capped here (Android, at × 1.5, was).
- EXP-09 fails on iOS as on Android (two setting stores); it passes on the web.
- **EXP-45 passes on iOS:** Devanagari is extractable, because the native font is KohinoorDevanagari, not a variable font. Android outlined it (fail).
- Several files: one share sheet with all files ("7 Images"), so the WebKit multi-download problem of the web does not apply.
- EXP-33, EXP-39 (counting) and EXP-42 (Save to Files) were checked on Android with native taps. Here they need the owner. The EXP-26/27 toast was checked on Android and not here.
